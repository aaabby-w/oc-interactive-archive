# Residence scene brief

## References and ownership

- The existing residence determines the room layout, furniture, cat anchors and observation-board presentation.
- `annac777/xi4u` supplies rendering and staging principles only: an orthographic miniature, a camera that remains above the ground plane, a broad shadow-receiving environment, restrained seasonal colors, and physical scenery under shared light.
- The residence does not copy xi4u's garden plan, architecture, title system, seasons or characters.
- The six requested background tracks are redistributed under the upstream repository's MIT license with its copyright notice preserved in `public/audio/xi4u/LICENSE`.

## Composition

The three dominant silhouettes are the room floor and two cutaway walls, the large physical window opening, and a continuous outdoor horizon behind and around the room. The room occupies roughly two thirds of the home view. Exterior hills and trees remain subordinate, but extend beyond the window so orbiting the camera never reveals a flat weather card.

The camera stays orthographic and above the ground plane. Rotation may reveal the rear and side construction, but the polar range must never pass under the landscape or expose the underside of the residence. A large exterior ground plane continues beneath the room and receives the same directional shadows as the architecture.

## Depth and lighting

The window consists of casing, sill, mullions and transparent glass. Behind it are real 3D hills, trees, clouds, celestial bodies and weather particles. The exterior objects occupy several depth bands and cast or receive shadows where physically plausible. Day, night and storm states change the shared background, fog, light and material palette instead of swapping a flat image.

## Interaction and motion

- Pointer and touch rotation remain observational; visitors cannot control the resident or cat.
- Orbit is limited to above-ground views, with zoom bounds retained.
- Weather keeps its existing dwell schedule. Rain moves through an exterior volume, while clear states move clouds slowly.
- Reduced motion keeps the environment static.
- Music starts only after a user gesture, loops through the licensed six-track playlist, and remains truthfully controlled by the existing music button.
