# Product

## Definition

An immersive, editorial website for original characters and their world. Visitors should feel that they are entering a living archive rather than opening a database.

## Audience promise

- Discover a character through atmosphere before reading facts.
- Move between characters, world material, archives, images, and later residences without losing a coherent sense of place.
- Receive a first-class experience on desktop and mobile.

## Product principles

1. Art direction before spectacle. A still screenshot must be convincing before motion is added.
2. Content before implementation detail. Characters and world entries are data, not one-off React pages.
3. Observation before control. A future Residence lets visitors witness and lightly interact; it is not a player-controlled game.
4. Progressive richness. A readable static experience exists beneath animation, sensors, and WebGL.
5. Replaceable identity. Until final branding exists, temporary names and marks must be clearly isolated and easy to replace.

## Current decisions

- Working name: unset; UI uses `未命名档案 / UNTITLED ARCHIVE` as an explicit placeholder.
- Logo: unset; a simple eclipse glyph is temporary.
- Tagline: unset; no fabricated marketing slogan is shown.
- Language: Chinese-led bilingual typography; no `/zh` or `/en` routes yet.
- Hero media: edge-to-edge temporary environment artwork. Official character art will replace it later without changing the page contract.
- Theme: light mineral-white and mint is the default; a dark counterpart is available through one-click switching.

## Not in the current phase

WebGL, Live2D Cubism, 3D rooms, autonomous-character simulation, device orientation, full archive templates, and deployment. A first-pass scroll story and mouse follower are now implemented by explicit request.
